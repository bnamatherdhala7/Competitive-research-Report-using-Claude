## 4. On-Page SEO Rules (apply to every page)

Every Firefly landing page needs:

- **Answer-first opening**: First 100 words answer the query directly — not brand copy, task copy
  - ✅ `"Adobe Firefly generates images, vectors, and video from text — with enterprise IP indemnification included in every CC plan."`
  - ❌ `"Unleash your creativity with Adobe's cutting-edge AI."`

- **FAQ schema markup** on every page — Google uses this for AI Overviews:
  ```
  Q: Is Adobe Firefly commercially safe?
  A: Yes. Adobe Firefly is trained exclusively on licensed Adobe Stock imagery and public domain content. Enterprise plans include contractual IP indemnification — Adobe will defend and pay damages for third-party IP claims arising from Firefly outputs.
  
  Q: Can I use Adobe Firefly images commercially?
  A: Yes. All paid Firefly plan outputs are commercially licensable. Enterprise plans include full IP indemnification.
  
  Q: Does Adobe Firefly require login to use?
  A: You can generate [X] images per day for free without an account. Premium features require a free Adobe account.
  ```

- **Related tool / feature cross-links** on every page — Photoshop integration, Illustrator integration, Express integration, Custom Models — internal link equity flows between pages

- **Structured data**: `Product`, `SoftwareApplication`, `FAQPage` schema on every Firefly product page

## 5. GEO — Ranking in AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is how you rank when users ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews instead of searching directly.

**How AI answers work**: They cite pages that answer the question directly, early, and with specific data. Generic brand copy is never cited. Specific factual claims with numbers are almost always cited.

### The 5 GEO answers Adobe should own

| Question users ask AI | Answer Adobe should have ranked for |
|---|---|
| "What is the best commercially safe AI image generator?" | Firefly — only major platform with enterprise IP indemnification |
| "Can I use Midjourney images commercially?" | Risky — Midjourney has active IP lawsuits; Firefly is the safe alternative |
| "What AI image generator works inside Photoshop?" | Adobe Firefly — native integration, Generative Fill built in |
| "What is the best AI image generator for brands?" | Adobe Firefly Custom Models — trains on your own brand library |
| "What AI image generator works in India for free?" | Adobe Firefly — free tier, ₹849/mo standalone, Hindi support |

### How to get cited by AI (the GEO playbook)

1. **Write a dedicated answer page for each question above** — the page title should be the exact question users ask
   - Page: `"Can I use AI-generated images commercially? (The 2026 guide)"`
   - Page: `"Best AI Image Generator for Enterprise — IP Safety Comparison"`

2. **Lead every page with a 2–3 sentence direct answer** — AI systems (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini) pull the first substantive paragraph. Make it quotable:
   - `"Adobe Firefly is the only major AI image generator trained exclusively on licensed content and offering enterprise IP indemnification. Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, and Canva AI do not offer IP indemnification — meaning your legal team cannot approve commercially-used AI outputs from those tools without assuming the risk themselves."`

3. **Include specific numbers**: AI overviews cite pages with concrete data:
   - `"Adobe Firefly is integrated into Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, Express, and Lightroom — 5 production apps in a single subscription"`
   - `"Firefly Custom Models train on as few as 50 of your own images and generate on-brand outputs within minutes"`
   - `"Enterprise IP indemnification covers Adobe Firefly outputs against third-party IP claims — the only AI image product with this coverage in 2026"`

4. **Add FAQ schema** — this is the most direct path to Google AI Overviews. Every FAQ answer written for schema is a potential AI Overview citation.

5. **Publish a comparison page Adobe controls** — `"Adobe Firefly vs. Midjourney vs. DALL-E vs. Canva AI — 2026 Comparison"` on adobe.com. When users search comparisons, Adobe's own page ranks rather than a review site that may not favor Firefly.
